What is WIDERA?

Strengthening the ERA is a general objective of Horizon Europe, backed by a dedicated programme part with two components.

Widening participation and spreading excellence

Helps close the research and innovation gap across Europe by bridging lower-performing countries and regions into the ERA and giving them fair access to excellence.

Reforming and enhancing the European R&I system

Supports the four priority areas of the Pact for Research and Innovation and the ERA Policy Agendas, shaping every WIDERA work programme in turn.

€3.2 billion

is the approximate budget for the WIDERA programme part across 2021–2027 — funding both components above.
